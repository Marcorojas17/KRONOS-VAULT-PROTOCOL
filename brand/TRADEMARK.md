This policy covers:

- The name **"Kronos"** as a project name.
- The acronym **"KTP"**.
- The compound identifiers **"Kronos Vault"** and
  **"Kronos Vault Protocol"**.
- The retained expansion **"Kronos Trust Protocol"** used for the KTP
  specification series.
- The reserved name **"Kronos Vault Services"** for any future
  optional commercial services.
- The official logo and any visual identity files in `brand/assets/`.

## `// 03` — WHAT THIS POLICY DOES NOT RESTRICT

This policy does **NOT** restrict:

- The use of the specification under CC-BY-4.0.
- The implementation of KTP-001 in any product or service.
- The commercial exploitation of any implementation.
- The redistribution of the specification with attribution.

  or technical contexts.
