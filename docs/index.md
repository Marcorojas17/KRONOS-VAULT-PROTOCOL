## What is KTP

**Kronos Vault Protocol (KTP)** is an open standard for representing,
sealing, and verifying interoperable digital evidence. It solves the
lack of a common format to prove the **existence**, **integrity**, and
**logical state** of a digital asset — without depending on a central
authority.

## What it is NOT

```text
  ✗  KTP does NOT certify authorship
  ✗  KTP does NOT determine legal ownership
  ✗  KTP does NOT prove identity
  ✗  KTP does NOT replace legal, forensic, or notarial services
  ✗  KTP does NOT require any central server or account
  ✗  KTP is NOT a commercial product or certification body
```

What KTP proves: **existence · integrity · logical state.**
What the law decides: **identity · authorship · ownership · validity.**
